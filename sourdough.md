# Sourdough — 70% Hydration

Makes 1 loaf. Dough total 945 g.

## Levain

Use a young levain only: 4–8 hours after feeding, still rising, before it peaks.

- Bubbly and domed, roughly doubled, smells mildly tangy.
- Not ready: flat, few bubbles, under 4 hours old. Wait.
- Too far: past 8 hours, top has collapsed or gone concave, smells sharp or boozy. Don't use it. Feed again and wait for the next 4–8 hour window.

A young levain gives a milder, less sour loaf and stronger rise.

## Formula

| Ingredient               | Grams | Baker's % |
|--------------------------|------:|----------:|
| Bread flour              |   450 |     90.0% |
| Whole wheat flour        |    50 |     10.0% |
| Water                    |   310 |     62.0% |
| Young levain (100% hydration) | 100 |  20.0% |
| Salt                     |    10 |      2.0% |
| Water, held back for salt |   25 |      5.0% |
| **Dough total**          | **945** |  189.0% |

Water total 335 g (310 + 25). Counting the 50 g flour and 50 g water in the levain, overall hydration is 385 / 550 = 70%.

## Method

Mix (stand mixer, dough hook)
1. Check the levain against the window above before you start.
2. Flours, 310 g water, and levain in the bowl. Low speed just until no dry flour is left. Cover, rest 20 min.
3. Add salt and the 25 g held-back water. Low speed about 2 min, until the water is absorbed.
4. Rest 20 min. Low speed under 1 min.
5. Rest 20 min. Low speed under 1 min.
   These short mixer sets replace stretch-and-folds. Each set should leave the dough smoother and more elastic. If it still tears or slumps after the second set, do a third.

Bulk
6. Into a lightly oiled tub. Leave it alone, no more folds.
7. Let it rise until about 50% bigger, domed, and bubbly at the edges. Usually 4–6 h at 24 °C (75 °F).

Shape and proof
8. Pre-shape into a round, rest 20–30 min. Final shape, into a floured banneton seam-side up.
9. Cover and refrigerate 8–16 h.

Bake
10. Preheat oven with Dutch oven inside to 250 °C (480 °F).
11. Turn out, score, bake covered 20 min.
12. Uncover, lower to 230 °C (450 °F), bake 20–25 min more until deep brown.
13. Cool at least 1 h before cutting.
