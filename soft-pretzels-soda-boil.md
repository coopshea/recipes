# Soft Pretzel Dough — Baking Soda Boil, Bites + Doner Flatbreads

Original recipe, salt corrected to 2%. Party batch = 3000 g, mixed as two 1500 g stand-mixer batches.
Makes: 12 brat buns + 30 pretzel bites + 18 doner flatbreads.

## Soda bath and wash (buns and bites)

| Item                | Amount |
|---------------------|--------|
| Water               | 2130 g (9 cups) |
| Baking soda         | 75 g (1/3 cup) |
| Egg wash            | 2 eggs + 30 g water |
| Butter for brushing | 45 g, melted |
| Coarse pretzel salt | ~50 g |

## Method

Day 1
1. Whisk warm water, sugar, yeast. Rest 5 min.
2. Add flour, melted butter, salt. Knead 8–10 min in the mixer to windowpane.
3. Bulk 2 h at room temp, until about 1.5×. Combine both batches in one tub.
4. Fold to degas, oil lightly, cover, fridge overnight.

Day 2, buns and bites
5. Divide cold. Roll the bite dough into 2 cm ropes and cut 3 cm pieces. Shape 12 bun logs. Proof until puffy and a poke springs back slowly, ~60 min from cold.
6. Boil bites 20 s total in batches of ~10, buns 30 s per side. Drain on a rack. Egg wash, coarse salt, bake. Brush with butter out of the oven.

Day 2, flatbreads
7. Divide 18 × 80 g, round into tight balls, set on an oiled tray, cover, back in the fridge.
8. Pull 60–90 min before cooking. Press and stretch each to ~18 cm.
9. Cook in the Ooni or cast iron. Stack in a clean towel so they stay soft. Hold in a 90°C / 200°F oven.
