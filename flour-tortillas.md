# Flour Tortillas — Tortillas de Harina

Adapted from [Mexico in My Kitchen's flour tortillas](https://www.mexicoinmykitchen.com/flour-tortillas-de-harina/).

Makes 10 tortillas, 25 cm (10 in). Dough total 638 g, divided 10 × 64 g.

## Formula

| Ingredient               | Original       | Grams | Baker's % |
|--------------------------|----------------|------:|----------:|
| All-purpose flour        | 2 1/2 cups     |   325 |    100.0% |
| Salt                     | 1 teaspoon     |     6 |      1.8% |
| Baking powder            | 1/2 teaspoon   |     2 |      0.6% |
| Shortening or lard       | 1/3 cup        |    65 |     20.0% |
| Hot water                | 1 cup          |   240 |     73.8% |
| **Dough total**          |                | **638** |  196.3% |

Plus a little extra flour for rolling. Hold back some of the water in humid weather.

## Divide

10 × 64 g balls. If you held back water, weigh the finished dough and divide by 10.

## Method

Dough
1. Mix flour, baking powder, and salt. Cut in the fat with a fork, pastry blender, or fingers until it looks like coarse meal.
2. Add the hot water a little at a time until a dough forms.
3. Turn out and knead a couple of minutes until smooth.

Divide and rest
4. Divide 10 × 64 g. Roll each under your palm on a lightly floured surface into a small disk (testales).
5. Cover with a damp towel, plastic, or parchment. Rest 30–45 min. Don't skip this; it relaxes the gluten so the disks roll out without springing back.

Roll
6. Heat an ungreased griddle, cast iron, or comal over medium.
7. Use as little flour on the surface as you can. Too much makes dry tortillas.
8. Press the ball flat. From the center, roll forward, stopping short of the edge, then back toward you, stopping short of the edge.
9. Rotate 45° and repeat. Flip occasionally. Keep going until it's thin and about 25 cm across.

Cook
10. Onto the hot griddle. After 20–30 s, bubbles form and the bottom gets light golden spots.
11. Flip. Cook ~20 s, more bubbles.
12. Flip again. It should puff, ~10 s.
13. Wrap in a kitchen towel. Stack as you go.

## Calories

Source says 147 kcal each. By weight it's closer to ~175 kcal per tortilla (flour ~1180 + fat ~585 kcal ÷ 10).
