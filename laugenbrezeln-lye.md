# Laugenbrezeln — Lye Dip, 52% Hydration

Adapted from [Dirndl Kitchen's German pretzel recipe](https://dirndlkitchen.com/german-pretzel-recipe/).

Party batch: 1600 g dough, kneaded by hand. Makes 16 pretzels at 100 g.

## Formula

| Ingredient                 | Baker's % | 1× ref (g) | Party batch (g) | Spec (Laugenbrezel) | Status |
|----------------------------|----------:|-----------:|----------------:|---------------------|--------|
| Bread flour (or AP)        |    100.0% |        540 |             990 | 100%                | OK |
| Water, ~25°C               |     52.0% |        281 |             515 | 50–55%              | OK |
| Salt                       |      2.0% |         11 |              20 | 1.8–2.2%            | OK |
| Butter or lard, soft       |      5.0% |         27 |              50 | 3–6%                | OK |
| Brown sugar or barley malt |      2.0% |         11 |              20 | 1–3%                | OK |
| Active dry yeast           |     0.75% |          4 |               7 | 0.7–1.5% ADY        | OK (2 h bulk + overnight fridge) |
| **Dough total**            |    161.8% |        874 |        **1602** |                     | |

Malt: use non-diastatic barley malt syrup (25 g) or dry malt extract (20 g) in place of the brown sugar. Avoid diastatic malt powder; US flour is already malted and the overnight retard would make the dough slack.

## Lye bath

| Item                        | Amount | Notes |
|-----------------------------|-------:|-------|
| Cold water                  | 2000 g | wide glass, stainless, or HDPE container, never aluminum |
| Food-grade sodium hydroxide |   80 g | 4%. Add lye to water, never water to lye |
| Coarse pretzel salt         | ~70 g  | |

Safety: nitrile gloves, eye protection, ventilation. Kids and pets out of the kitchen while dipping.

## Method

Day 1 (hand knead)
1. Dissolve sugar and yeast in the water. Add flour, salt, soft butter. Mix to a shaggy mass. Cover, rest 20 min.
2. Knead 5 min, rest 10 min. Knead 5 min, rest 10 min. Knead 5 min. The rests do the work a mixer would. Do not add water.
3. Dough should be smooth, tight, and stretch thin without tearing easily. Full windowpane is not required; the overnight fridge finishes development.
4. Bulk 2 h at room temp, until about 1.5×. Fold, cover, fridge overnight.

Day 2
5. Out of fridge 30 min before dividing. Divide 16 × 100 g.
6. Pre-roll each to 20 cm, rest 10 min. Roll to 55–65 cm, thick belly, thin arms. Twist twice, press ends onto belly.
7. Proof 45 min at room temp, then uncovered in fridge at least 45 min to skin over.
8. Dip cold, 3–5 s. Drain. Onto parchment or silicone mats.
9. Salt immediately while wet. One deep score across the belly.
10. Bake 210°C / 410°F, 15–18 min until deep mahogany.

## Calories

~255 kcal per pretzel. Batch ~4060 kcal.
